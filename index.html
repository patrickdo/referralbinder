This guide is intended for ambulatory radiology referral use only and does not include exams performed at our affiliated Mercy Hospitals.

Pre-MRI eGFR testing is ONLY required within 6 weeks of the MRI for patients with any of these conditions:
  • History of kidney disease in adults age 18 or older, including a renal tumor or kidney transplant.
  • Solid organ transplant other than a kidney transplant.
  • History of severe liver disease, liver transplant, or pending liver transplant.
Pre-CT eGFR testing is ONLY required within 6 weeks of a contrast-enhanced CT for patients with a personal history of renal disease, including:
  • Chronic kidney disease (CKD)
  • Remote history of acute kidney injury (AKI)
  • Dialysis
  • Kidney surgery or ablation
  • Albuminuria
