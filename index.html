This guide is intended for ambulatory radiology referral use only and does not include exams performed at our affiliated Mercy Hospitals.

Pre-MRI lab assessment of eGFR is only required within 6 weeks of the MRI for patients with the following conditions:

  • History of 'kidney disease' (including renal tumor or transplant) as an adult 18 years and older.
  • Solid organ transplant (excluding renal transplant, which is covered above).
  • History of severe hepatic disease, liver transplant, or pending liver transplant.
